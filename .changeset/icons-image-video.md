Add nine icons for image and video editing: `FlipHorizontalIcon`, `FlipVerticalIcon`, `CropIcon` and `FitIcon` (zoom to fit, also for contain and cover), and `TrimStartIcon`, `TrimEndIcon` (trim to the playhead), `FadeInIcon`, `FadeOutIcon` and `SnapIcon` (a magnet). Their names join `IconName`, so tools can refer to them as `"flip-horizontal"`, `"fit"`, `"trim-start"` and so on.
